## Project Abstract

StudySync is a web-based academic workload and assignment management system designed for college students. The application will allow students to organize their courses and assignments, track due dates, estimate the amount of time needed to complete their work, set priority levels, and monitor assignment completion. StudySync will also provide a dashboard that summarizes upcoming and overdue assignments and uses a prioritization system to recommend which assignment the student should focus on next based on factors such as due date, estimated completion time, and priority level. The goal of StudySync is to provide college students with a simple and organized way to manage their academic workload and make better decisions about how to prioritize their time.
